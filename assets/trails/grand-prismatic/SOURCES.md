# Grand Prismatic trail imagery

The nearby feature images are local copies of public-domain photographs from the
National Park Service NPGallery. They are used as contextual visuals in the
prototype's map cards.

- `excelsior.jpg` — [Aerial view of Excelsior Geyser and Grand Prismatic Spring](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/3CD0B5E3-1DD8-B71B-0B6581AD308BC259), NPS/Jim Peaco.
- `midway.jpg` — [Busy boardwalks in the Midway Geyser Basin](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/149406e1-f45b-45a0-b890-b3ab91730621), NPS/Neal Herbert.
- `grand-prismatic-spring.jpg` — [Visitors on boardwalk at Grand Prismatic Spring in Midway Geyser Basin](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/8ecfa1e0-9fd7-4ae5-b9b5-98d35fe84033), NPS/Jim Peaco.
- `goose-lake-context.jpg` — [Yellowstone Lake, just east of Steamboat Point in Sedge Bay](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/00572d29-1dd8-b71b-0b81-a93433497af9), NPS/Jim Peaco. NPGallery did not return a Goose Lake-specific Yellowstone photograph, so this is intentionally treated as a broader Yellowstone lake context image rather than an exact location photo.
